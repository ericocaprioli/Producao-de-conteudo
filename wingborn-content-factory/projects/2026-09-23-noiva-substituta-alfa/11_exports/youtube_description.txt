Her stepmother traded her life for a mountain pass — and sent her north under her stepsister's name.
But the old dragon no one could touch was waiting in the yard, with a torn wing and a long memory.

Maelle has been the forgotten seamstress of her father's house. When a daughter must be sent as hostage to the dragon citadel of Cold Reach, her stepmother dresses her in her stepsister's cloak and tells her never to say her real name. At the gate, Maelle learns what no one at home dared to mention: if the treaty breaks, the hostage pays for it. And her stepmother already knows the treaty will break.

With nothing but her mother's bone needle case, a mocked one-armed wing-mender and a dragon who burns anyone who touches her, Maelle has one winter to mend what everyone else has given up on — before she has to decide whose name she will die under.

A calm, cinematic dark fantasy story for quiet nights. Stay until the end, and tell us where you are listening from.
